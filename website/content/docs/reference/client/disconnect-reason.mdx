Why a client's connection to the gateway ended, or why an attempt to connect failed. Carried by [`DisconnectInfo`](/docs/reference/client/disconnect-info) in [`NebulaClient.Disconnected`](/docs/reference/client/nebula-client#disconnected) and [`NebulaClient.LastDisconnect`](/docs/reference/client/nebula-client#lastdisconnect). Branch on it to choose what to show: a network problem on the player's side, a server that is down, or a decision the server made.

| <span id="timeout"></span>`Timeout` | `1` | The connection was up and the gateway stopped answering for longer than the transport's disconnect timeout: the gateway, or the network between, went away. |
