How late a client's world-state streams arrive, for the adaptive part of the render delay ([`NebulaConfig.AdaptiveInterpolationDelay`](/docs/reference/core/nebula-config#adaptiveinterpolationdelay)). Each packet's lateness is how many ticks older it is than the tick the client's clock expected to arrive at that moment; on a clean stream it hovers around zero, and a worker that runs behind, or a link that bunches packets, shows up as positive values. The render delay must cover the lateness of nearly every packet or the interpolator runs past its newest sample and extrapolates: this keeps a window of samples per stream (per worker, since one slow worker's entities are a small share of the packets) and reports the highest per-stream percentile.

Pure and allocation-free after warm-up, so it is tested on its own.
