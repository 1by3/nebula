The call ids a worker has applied, so a call that arrives twice runs once (design D5). Bounded two ways: at most [`Capacity`](#capacity) ids are remembered, oldest forgotten first, and an id older than [`TtlTicks`](#ttlticks) ticks is forgotten on the next [`Expire`](#expire). A duplicate of a forgotten call would be applied again; the bounds say how long "at most once" is enforced for.
