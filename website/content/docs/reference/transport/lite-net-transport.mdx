[`ITransport`](/docs/reference/transport/i-transport) over LiteNetLib. Sequenced maps to `DeliveryMethod.Sequenced`, everything else to `DeliveryMethod.ReliableOrdered`. Two LiteNetLib channels are used so a large reliable message cannot head-of-line block the transform stream. Events are queued by LiteNetLib and drained on the caller's thread inside [`Poll`](#poll).
