### Sequenced [#sequenced]

```csharp
public readonly bool Sequenced;
```

For a `Kind.Data` event: whether the packet arrived on the [`Delivery.Sequenced`](/docs/reference/transport/delivery#sequenced) channel, where a late packet may be lost, instead of the reliable one. False when the transport does not say.
