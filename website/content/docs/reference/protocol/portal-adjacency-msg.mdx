[`MsgId.PortalAdjacency`](/docs/reference/protocol/msg-id#portaladjacency) (`docs/portals.md` D30, D45): a link the sender drives, as a worker that holds the other side of its band needs it. The portal record carries the near face, the aperture and the band; the far face is the link's, which a template shares with the portal but a fixed far side may not. [`FarContainers`](#farcontainers) are the containers of the far scope the far band touches, the relation that makes a body in the band of one face a ghost on the worker that owns the other. A removal withdraws it.
