One entity of the far relevance tier (`docs/interest-management.md` §16, NEB-388): a large or important entity seen beyond the normal interest radius as a pose and nothing else. No variables, no RPCs, no sync state, no container: the position is **absolute in the entity's scope, in double** (the space region keys and the gateway's own positions are in, converted out of every physics frame the entity stands in), so a receiver needs no container row and no frame to place it, and a coordinate 106 m out keeps sub-millimetre resolution on the wire. Rotation is in the same space (smallest-three, 4 bytes, about 0.1 degree), velocity in half floats (m/s).
