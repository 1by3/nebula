[`MsgId.PortalHandoff`](/docs/reference/protocol/msg-id#portalhandoff) (`docs/portals.md` D44, D45): the links of one portal, sent by the worker that is releasing the portal's near container to the worker that is taking it, together with the release. Nothing else about a link needs to travel: its far scope and its readiness are worked out again from the portal's own content and the leases.
