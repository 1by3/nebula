The contents of an entity's maps kept as opaque bytes, by a party that relays them without knowing their types: the gateway, so a late joiner is sent the current contents (docs/replicated-collections.md D6). Seeded from a full section, updated by delta sections, encoded back into a full section on demand (once per change).
