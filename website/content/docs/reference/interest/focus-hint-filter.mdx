Validation of [`ClientFocusHintMsg`](/docs/reference/protocol/client-focus-hint-msg), on the gateway, before a policy ever sees a hint. A hint is an input and never authority: a client that sends a point on the other side of the map, or a thousand points a second, must not be able to make the gateway stream it the world. Non-finite values are dropped, the rate is capped at [`InterestSettings.HintMaxHz`](/docs/reference/interest/interest-settings#hintmaxhz), and the point is clamped to [`InterestSettings.HintMaxDistance`](/docs/reference/interest/interest-settings#hintmaxdistance) from the pawn unless the policy allows free foci.
