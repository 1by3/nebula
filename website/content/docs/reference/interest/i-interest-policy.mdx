The game's say in what a client hears about. Both methods are called by the gateway, per client, at [`InterestSettings.EvalHz`](/docs/reference/interest/interest-settings#evalhz); neither may block or allocate per call if the mesh is to stay quiet.

### Authorize [#authorize]

```csharp
bool Authorize(in InterestClient client, in InterestEntity entity);
```

The security filter, asked before an entity may enter or stay in the set (team, fog of war). Returning false removes the entity at once, with no linger: this is a boundary, not a distance.
