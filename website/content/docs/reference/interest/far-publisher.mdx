The worker's half of the far tier: which gateways hear about each far entity, and when. Pure bookkeeping over a [`RegionPublisher`](/docs/reference/interest/region-publisher)'s foci, so the Unity worker and the service tests' fake worker publish it the same way.

Each tick the host calls [`Begin`](#begin), then [`Offer`](#offer) for every far entity it is authoritative for whose [`IsDue`](#isdue) says so (with the entry filled in: its pose absolute in its scope), then [`End`](#end). What to send is in [`Out`](#out): one mask and one entry each, to be grouped by mask by the host.

The match runs at [`EvalInterval`](#evalinterval): a far entity reaches a gateway with a focus of the entity's scope's own space (frame key 0) within its far radius. Every client with a pawn has one: its pawn's focus in the scope, or the enclosing focus the gateway adds around a pawn on a planet (`docs/container-tree.md` D18), so the tier crosses frames without converting foci on the worker. A gateway that gains the entity is sent an entry at once; one that loses it is sent [`FarEntryKind.Gone`](/docs/reference/protocol/far-entry-kind#gone). In between, each entity is sent at its own rate.

One far entity this tick, with its entry filled in ([`FarEntryKind.State`](/docs/reference/protocol/far-entry-kind#state), position absolute in its scope). `scopeSalt` is [`RegionKeys.SaltOf`](/docs/reference/interest/region-keys#saltof) of its scope: the foci it is matched with are the ones collected in that scope's own space.

### End [#end]

End a tick: an entity tracked but neither offered nor kept has left the tier (its far radius was set to 0, it boarded something). The gateways that held it are sent [`FarEntryKind.Gone`](/docs/reference/protocol/far-entry-kind#gone).
