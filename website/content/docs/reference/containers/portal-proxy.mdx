Marks a collider Nebula made to carry one side of a portal into the other (`docs/portals.md` §6): a kinematic copy of a body that touches an open link's band (D26), a static copy of the other side's geometry in the throat (D27), or ghost held only as a proxy on a worker that does not simulate its scope (D29). It lives on [`NebulaConfig.PortalProxyLayer`](/docs/reference/core/nebula-config#portalproxylayer) and names the entity it stands for, so a hit can be attributed and a query or a game's trigger can skip it ([`Is`](#is)). The aperture colliders (D28) carry it too, with no authority.

A proxy is not an entity and is not replicated; it exists on the process that built it, in the physics scene of the side it was built for. A game must keep [`NebulaConfig.PortalProxyLayer`](/docs/reference/core/nebula-config#portalproxylayer) free of its own objects and out of its own layer masks (raycasts that should not hit a copy of a crate on the far side of a wall), and let the layer collide with whatever bodies collide with.
