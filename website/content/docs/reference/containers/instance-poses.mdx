The instances this process holds whose own space is turned against their host scope's (NEB-426, `docs/scope-frames.md` D12): built upright in their own space and posed at a door that is not upright in the host (a door on a sphere). Learned from the instances' lease rows ([`InstanceContainerInfo.HasPose`](/docs/reference/protocol/instance-container-info#haspose)) when their first container is registered here, and forgotten with their last. A point p of the instance's space is `origin + rotation × (p − origin)` in its host's.
