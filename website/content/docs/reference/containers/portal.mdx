A way between two scopes: an opening in a wall, a hatch, a gate, a door to a room of its own (`docs/portals.md`). It stands in the near side's content, at a [`NearFace`](#nearface) fixed in a container, and leads to a far side that is a fixed scope ([`SetFixedFarSide`](#setfixedfarside)) or a template, one far scope per party ([`FarTemplate`](#fartemplate)).

The game opens it for a party with [`Open`](#open), naming the clients that will cross (its members), and closes it with [`Close`](#close). Each party has its own [`PortalLink`](/docs/reference/containers/portal-link) with a state and, while it is not open, the reason ([`PortalLink.Wait`](/docs/reference/containers/portal-link#wait)). A link is open once the far scope is active and its content loaded on the worker that owns it, and every member's client holds the far window and has said so: readiness is standing, held for as long as the link is open, so crossing loads nothing and holds nothing at the face. Everything runs on the worker that owns the portal's container.

An entity crosses when it passes through the aperture, the opening on the face's z = 0 plane: anything simulated here (a player's pawn, a crate, a projectile, a vehicle with its passengers) goes through an open link on the tick its root ends [`CrossDepth`](#crossdepth) beyond the plane, or, for a body that moves further than that in a step, on the tick before the step that would carry it through. It keeps its identity and carries its pose, its velocities and its history, turned by the link when the faces are turned against each other. One the link does not let through is stopped at the face ([`Refused`](#refused)). [`ResolveCompanions`](#resolvecompanions) names entities that cross with it. The hooks [`PartyOf`](#partyof), [`CanEnter`](#canenter) and [`CanObserve`](#canobserve) are the game's policy.

### CloseHoldSeconds [#closeholdseconds]

```csharp

```

How long a link stays [`PortalState.Closing`](/docs/reference/protocol/portal-state#closing) at least once [`Close`](#close) is called, in seconds, even with its band empty (`docs/portals.md` D9). A closing link is still observed, so every observer keeps drawing its far side (D40): this is the time a game needs for whatever it shows closing over the aperture (a door's leaves sliding shut, a shutter, a fade) to finish while the far side is still there to be seen behind it. Nothing enters a closing link meanwhile; bodies already straddling finish as usual. 0, the default, closes on the first tick the band is empty. Kept below [`CloseTimeoutSeconds`](#closetimeoutseconds).

### ResolveCompanions [#resolvecompanions]

```csharp
public Action<NetworkIdentity, List<NetworkIdentity>> ResolveCompanions;
```

Server-side: fill the list with the entities that must cross together with the one crossing, its companions: an item it holds, a pet, a cart it tows, an escort (`docs/portals.md` D14). Called on the worker that simulates the crossing entity, once a tick while it is near the face, with an empty list. A companion moves with its leader and does not cross on its own while it is one. The group commits in one step on the tick the leader crosses, each member keeping its identity and arriving at its own pose mapped through the link. A companion that cannot cross is left behind ([`CompanionLeftBehind`](#companionleftbehind)); with the far side warm, a companion never waits for a preparation of its own.

### Crossed [#crossed]

```csharp
public event Action<NetworkIdentity, Portal, ulong, ulong> Crossed;
```

Raised after an entity crossed through this portal, on the worker that committed it and on every client that holds the entity when its container change arrives: the entity, this portal, the scope it left and the scope it entered (so `to` is the far scope when it went in and the near scope when it came out). A game's own heading bookkeeping turns with [`Portals.Turn`](/docs/reference/containers/portals#turn)`(from, to)` here. On a client the event is raised only where this component exists; [`Portals.Crossed`](/docs/reference/containers/portals#crossed) is raised wherever the crossing is seen. Handlers run inside the worker's tick (or the client's frame) and must not throw.

### Refused [#refused]

```csharp
public event Action<NetworkIdentity, PortalLink, PortalRefusal> Refused;
```

Raised on this worker every time an entity reached the aperture and was not let through (`docs/portals.md` D17): the entity, the link it was refused by (null when it has no link here: its party never opened one, or the face has several parties' links open and nothing says whose it is, [`PortalRefusal.AmbiguousParty`](/docs/reference/containers/portal-refusal#ambiguousparty)), and why. The entity has been stopped at the face already: its root put back [`CrossDepth`](#crossdepth) short of the plane and the part of its velocity that pointed through removed, so a game can bounce, drop or destroy it. A body that keeps pushing against the aperture is refused on every tick it reaches it. Handlers run inside the worker's tick and must not throw.

### Open [#open]

```csharp
public PortalLink Open(string party, IEnumerable<ulong> members = null);
```

Open the portal for `party`: the link goes to [`PortalState.Preparing`](/docs/reference/protocol/portal-state#preparing) and the worker that owns the portal prepares the far scope and its content, asks the members' clients to hold the far window, and turns the link [`PortalState.Open`](/docs/reference/protocol/portal-state#open) when every condition holds (`docs/portals.md` D10). Call it on that worker. Opening a link that is already live adds the members; one that is closing opens again.

| Parameter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `party` | The party the far scope belongs to (D8). A fixed far side usually has one party, the empty string. |
| `members` | The client ids that will cross, and must be warm before the link opens. None is fine for a link only server-owned entities use. |

**Returns:** The party's link.

### Close [#close]

```csharp
public bool Close(string party);
```

Close the portal for `party`: the link goes to [`PortalState.Closing`](/docs/reference/protocol/portal-state#closing), waits for the band to be empty (at most [`CloseTimeoutSeconds`](#closetimeoutseconds)), and then turns [`PortalState.Closed`](/docs/reference/protocol/portal-state#closed). False when it has no live link.

### AddMember [#addmember]

```csharp
public bool AddMember(string party, ulong clientId);
```

Add a client to a live link's members (`docs/portals.md` D13). It warms on its own, without closing the link for the others: its wait is [`PortalWait.ClientWarming`](/docs/reference/protocol/portal-wait#clientwarming), and its own entities are refused at the aperture until it is warm.

### AddObserver [#addobserver]

```csharp
public bool AddObserver(string party, ulong clientId);
```

Let a client look through a party's link without being a member (`docs/portals.md` D11, D20): a bystander at a door, a friend watching a room. While the link is open and the client stands inside the near window it is sent the far window; when it stands in the far window it is sent the near one. It is not held to warm the far window, so the link never waits for it, and its own entities are not admitted by the default [`CanEnter`](#canenter). Members are observers already unless [`CanObserve`](#canobserve) says otherwise.

**Returns:** True when the client was not an observer of a live link before.

### Validate [#validate]

```csharp
public bool Validate(List<Collider> blockers = null);
```

Check that this side's own static colliders leave the cross-section clear for [`BandDepth`](#banddepth) beyond the face (`docs/portals.md` D27): the tunnel mouth a game builds so that a body straddling the face is not pushed out of the doorway by the mass behind it. Run it in the Editor (a portal validates itself when it is edited) and by hand; a link also checks both of its sides when it starts preparing. Whatever is in the way is logged. In play mode it checks the near side and, for each link whose far container this process holds, the far side; in the Editor, the near side in the open scene.

| Parameter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `blockers` | When given, filled with the colliders in the way. |

**Returns:** True when the cross-section is clear.
