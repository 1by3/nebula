One thing a portal query found (`docs/portals.md` D32): the scope it is in, the collider and the entity, how far along the cast it is, and its point in both the asker's space and the hit scope's. Spaces are the physics scenes' own coordinates (the scope's scene, or its physics frame's simulation space), in float and small by construction: a point is a face-relative offset carried through the link about the faces, never a bare translation (D37).
