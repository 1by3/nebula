Where a drawn scope stands against the **anchor**, the scope whose frame the local view's frame resolves to (`docs/portals.md` D40, [`Portals.Anchor`](/docs/reference/containers/portals#anchor)): the composition, in double and about the faces, of the link poses along the portals from the anchor to the scope. A point of the scope's own upright space is `ToOrigin + Turn × (p − FromOrigin)` in the anchor's. Used to draw only: simulation, prediction and every pose a game reads stay in each scope's own space.
