Where a runtime container goes and what kind of container it is: its parent, its box, its authority and whether it gets its own physics frame. This is what a lease row carries ([`LeaseInfo.Placement`](/docs/reference/control-plane/lease-info#placement)) and what [`NebulaWorker.RequestRuntimeContainer`](/docs/reference/worker/nebula-worker#requestruntimecontainer) and [`ContainerRegistry.RegisterRuntime`](/docs/reference/containers/container-registry#registerruntime) take.

A **root** (no [`ParentId`](#parentid)) is placed in its scope: [`Center`](#center) is absolute and kept in double, so a root far from the origin is placed exactly. A **child** is placed in its parent's frame: [`Center`](#center) is local to the parent's origin and the box is axis-aligned in the parent's frame, so it moves and turns with the parent. See `docs/container-tree.md`.

A `Bounds` converts to a root placement, so every call that took an absolute box still compiles.
