Who is asking a portal query (`docs/portals.md` D12, D32): the entity whose own colliders the cast skips, and the party whose link it goes through. A face with several parties' links casts into the asker's party's far scope. With no party named, the entity's party is its [`Portal.PartyOf`](/docs/reference/containers/portal#partyof) (by default its owner's identity); with neither, the face's one open link, when there is exactly one.
