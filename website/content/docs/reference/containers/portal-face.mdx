One side of a [`Portal`](/docs/reference/containers/portal) (`docs/portals.md` D2): a scope, the container the face is fixed in, and a pose, an absolute [`Double3`](/docs/reference/containers/double3) position in that scope and a rotation. The face's +z axis is the way from the near scope into the far one: in its axes, z \< 0 is the near side's region and z ≥ 0 the far side's.

As built, the pose is held in the scope's own absolute space (what [`ContainerRegistry.ToAbsolutePrecise`](/docs/reference/containers/container-registry#toabsoluteprecise) gives, out of any physics frame the container is in) rather than in the container's local space, which is the form every crossing already compares in; the container is kept for ownership, lookup and the network gateway's rows.
