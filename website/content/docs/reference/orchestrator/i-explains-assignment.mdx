A policy that can say why it moved what it moved. Unity's Mono has no default interface methods and a game's own [`IAssignmentPolicy`](/docs/reference/orchestrator/i-assignment-policy) must keep compiling, so explaining is a second interface the built-in [`CostBalancedAssignmentPolicy`](/docs/reference/orchestrator/cost-balanced-assignment-policy) implements rather than a member of the policy interface. The orchestrator logs and publishes the moves when the policy in force offers them.

What the last [`IAssignmentPolicy.Compute`](/docs/reference/orchestrator/i-assignment-policy#compute) could not relieve, hottest first. Empty when the mesh is balanced, when nothing is hot enough to be worth reporting, or when no utilization was measured.
