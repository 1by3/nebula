The mesh's recent log lines, kept on the orchestrator so one place holds what every process of the mesh said last: workers, gateways and the orchestrator's own shipper post batches to `POST /api/logs` (`{"lines":[{"at","role","instance","level","message"}]}`), and readers page through `GET /api/logs?since=<seq>&role=&instance=&limit=`, which answers the lines after `since` and the next cursor. A fixed number of lines is kept; older ones fall off. A machine that is deleted keeps its last lines here for as long as the buffer holds them, which is what makes a worker's last words readable after it is gone. Thread-safe: posts and reads happen on the dashboard's listener threads.
