Container cost

What each container costs the worker that holds it, measured. Sim is the NetworkTick time its own entities take; Out is what replicating them costs; Relay is owner state, which a gateway forwards to one client each. Dominant is whichever of the three is closest to its budget, and it is what a hot container needs fixing in: simulation wants a split or cheaper entities, replication wants tighter interest, relay wants more gateways. Full means the dominant component reached the capacity threshold: joins and transfers into that container go to the game's admission hook, which refuses them by default.
